Empowering Girls Through Education

By Nhyira Frempong · March 23 · 4 min read
← Back to Blog

Education is the most powerful tool we have to change the world. And when that tool is placed in the hands of a girl — a girl who has been told she doesn't matter, that her place is at home, that school is not for her — extraordinary things happen. Communities change. Families break cycles of poverty. Nations grow stronger.

At GNET, we are part of a growing global movement of organizations, initiatives, and individuals who believe the same thing: girls deserve to learn, and the world is better when they do.

We Are Not Alone — A Global Movement

GNET stands alongside some of the world's most impactful organizations fighting for girls' education. These are our fellow believers — organizations whose goals align deeply with our own, and whose work inspires everything we do.

Malala Fund

Founded by Nobel Peace Prize laureate Malala Yousafzai, the Malala Fund advocates for 12 years of free, quality education for every girl. Like GNET, they believe no girl should be denied school because of who she is or where she was born.

Girl Rising

Girl Rising uses the power of storytelling to change how the world values girls and their education. Their campaigns have reached millions, shifting cultural attitudes and inspiring action — exactly the kind of awareness GNET fights for every day.

CAMFED

The Campaign for Female Education supports girls in sub-Saharan Africa through school, into work, and onward as leaders. CAMFED's model of educating girls and then empowering them to give back to their communities mirrors GNET's own vision.

Room to Read

Room to Read focuses on literacy and gender equality in education across Asia and Africa. Their Girls' Education Program helps girls build the skills and confidence to stay in school and succeed — a goal GNET shares completely.

UNESCO Education

UNESCO's global education programs champion inclusive, quality education for all. Their focus on gender equality in education directly aligns with GNET's mission, and we are actively working toward partnership with their teams.

Plan International

Plan International works in over 75 countries to advance children's rights and equality for girls. Their "Girls Get Equal" campaign tackles the root causes that keep girls out of school — poverty, discrimination, and violence.

What Empowerment Really Means

Empowerment is not just a word. It is what happens when a girl who was told she couldn't — does anyway. It happens when she walks into a classroom for the first time. When she reads her first sentence. When she stands up and speaks her mind. When she graduates and becomes the first in her family to do so.

"One child, one teacher, one book, one pen can change the world." — Malala Yousafzai

GNET's approach to empowerment is rooted in three things: access, awareness, and action. We remove financial barriers so girls can access school. We run seminars so communities understand why girls' education matters. And we take action — showing up in the hardest-to-reach places, building partnerships, and refusing to give up.

Students Empowering Students

One of the most powerful things about GNET is that we are youth-led. We believe students have the power to teach, inspire, and uplift other students — even across oceans and cultural divides. That's why we launched our Student Teaches Students program, where young people record lessons on math, science, life skills, technology, and leadership, and share them with girls in underserved communities who have never had access to quality teaching.

When a student in Columbus, Ohio records a 15-minute lesson on algebra and a girl in rural Uganda watches it and learns — that is empowerment in its purest form. No borders. No barriers. Just knowledge, shared freely.

Join the Movement

You don't have to be a world leader or a billionaire to empower girls. You just have to care enough to act. Whether you teach a lesson, donate to support a girl's education, or simply share GNET's story — you are part of this movement.

We are GNET. And we believe every girl deserves the chance to learn, to lead, and to change the world.
